## Why it's different

Most bookkeepers treat an MSP like any other services business. We don't.

Recurring revenue, ticket-driven labor, and vendor pass-through costs can behave differently in an MSP. If your books don't reflect that, your numbers will lie to you. We build logical categorization and reporting so your accounting data reflects how you run.
